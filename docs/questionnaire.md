Hi! I'm doing a college project on why people stop using health and calorie apps. Please answer honestly. There are no right answers. Your name is optional and I'll only use a first name or initials.

**Consent (required):** I'm happy for my answers to be used, anonymously, in a college report. ☐ Yes

## How to use the answers
- Count how many people ticked each reason in Q4 (and note the single most annoying thing in Q5). Compare with the 116-review table in the report.
- Quote 2–3 short Q5 answers (anonymous) in Q1.
- Follow up with the people who said yes to Q11. Those conversations are the "talk to at least three real people" evidence.
- Report how many people responded. A small honest number is fine.
