# Interview 2: Jose (first name used with his permission)

Collected 31 Oct 2026 by WhatsApp message, written answers. Age 26, desk job, home-cooked breakfast and dinner, eats out or canteen for lunch. Answers are his own words.

**1. Which app, and why?** HealthifyMe. "I downloaded it because I have a desk job, was feeling super sluggish, and wanted to lose a few kgs before my cousin's wedding."

**2. How long?** "I was religious about it for exactly three weeks. Then I started skipping days, and by week five, I stopped completely."

**3. Why did you stop?** "Honestly, life just got busy and it felt like too much work. Once the motivation of the wedding passed, logging every single thing I put in my mouth felt like a part-time job. If I forgot to log lunch, by dinner I'd feel like the whole day's data was ruined, so I just gave up entirely."

**4. Most annoying thing?** "The pushy notifications! It would buzz my phone to remind me to log water or a snack when I was in the middle of stressful office meetings."

**5. Home-cooked Indian food?** "I used the manual search, and it was a nightmare for Indian food. If my mom made bhindi masala, the app would show 15 different versions with totally different calories. Also, my mom's 'one katori' of dal and the app's idea of a katori were probably completely different. It felt like I was always just guessing."

**6. Guilt or giving up?** "Oh, definitely. If I went out with friends and had pizza, the calorie bar would turn angry red. It honestly ruined my mood. It made me feel like I had already 'failed' for the day, which ironically made me just want to eat more junk food since the day was already ruined."

**7. Money, ads, pop-ups?** "Yes. Every time I opened the app, there was a giant pop-up pushing me to buy their premium diet coaching plan. It got really annoying having to close ads just to log an apple."

**8. Would you use a type-or-say app?** "100% yes. If I could just open the app and text 'had a plate of poha and a cutting chai' like I was messaging a friend, I would probably still use it. Searching and guessing portion sizes is what kills the habit."

**9. What would keep you using it?** "It needs to be as fast as sending a WhatsApp message, and it needs to be encouraging instead of making me feel guilty when I slip up."

**10. Age and food?** 26. Home-cooked breakfast and dinner; usually eats out or orders from the canteen for lunch.
