# Interview 3: Arnav (first name used with his permission)

Collected 31 Oct 2026 by WhatsApp message, written answers. Age 32, about 80% home-cooked meals. Answers are his own words.

**1. Which app, and why?** HealthifyMe. "I downloaded it after a routine health checkup showed my cholesterol was slightly elevated, so I wanted to start monitoring my daily diet."

**2. How long?** "About two months and stopped completely right around Diwali last year."

**3. Why did you stop?** "The festival season completely derailed me. There were too many sweets and heavy meals to track accurately. After missing four days of logging, I felt too disconnected from the habit to start over, so I just gave up and uninstalled it."

**4. Most annoying thing?** "The upsells. Every time I completed a meal log, it tried to sell me a consultation with a dietician or a metabolic blood test."

**5. Home-cooked Indian food?** "I used the search bar, and it was terrible for homemade Indian food. If my wife made a mixed vegetable gravy, I had no clue exactly how much oil or which specific veggies went into it. I'd just guess and pick a generic 'mixed veg curry' from the list, which felt completely inaccurate."

**6. Guilt or giving up?** "Absolutely. The app turns the calorie bar bright red when you go over your limit. If I knew we were ordering biryani for dinner, I wouldn't even open the app that evening because I didn't want to see the red warning and feel bad about it."

**7. Money, ads, pop-ups?** "Yes, the constant pop-ups for premium coaching plans were a huge distraction and made the app feel cluttered."

**8. Would you use a type-or-say app?** "I would definitely use that. The biggest barrier is the time it takes to search, select a portion size, and add each item individually. Just typing a normal sentence would solve the friction completely."

**9. What would keep you using it?** "It needs to forgive missed days without breaking streaks, and it shouldn't require me to weigh my food on a scale to get an accurate entry."

**10. Age and food?** 32; about 80% home-cooked.
