# Interview 1: Rizwan (first name used with his permission)

Collected 31 Oct 2026 by WhatsApp message, written answers. Early 20s, gym-goer, mostly home-cooked meals. Answers are his own words, lightly trimmed only for the question labels.

**1. Which apps, and why?** MyFitnessPal and HealthifyMe. "I started using them when I got serious about the gym and needed to track my protein intake and maintain a calorie surplus to bulk up."

**2. How long?** MyFitnessPal consistently for about a month, then started missing days, and stopped completely after maybe two months.

**3. Why did you stop?** "It just became a chore. Every time I sat down to eat, I had to pull out my phone, search for the food, and guess the portion size before my meal got cold. I eventually just memorized the rough protein and calorie estimates for my regular daily meals and gave up on the app."

**4. Most annoying thing?** "Sifting through the search results. If you search for something basic, you get 20 different user-submitted entries with wildly different macros, and you have to guess which one is accurate."

**5. Home-cooked Indian food?** "A headache. The apps usually ask for measurements in grams, but we serve food in katoris or ladles. If I had 3 rotis, a bowl of dal, and mixed sabzi, I had to guess how many grams of dal it was and how much oil was used in the sabzi. It was never accurate."

**6. Guilt or giving up?** "It didn't make me feel guilty, but it was definitely demotivating on cheat days. If I went out and had a heavy biryani, I'd often just skip logging it entirely because I didn't want to see the huge red warning bar telling me I ruined my macros for the day."

**7. Money, ads, pop-ups?** "Yes, the paywalls killed it for me. When MyFitnessPal locked the barcode scanner behind a premium subscription, that was a major reason I stopped. HealthifyMe also had a lot of pop-ups pushing their paid coaching plans."

**8. Would you use a type-or-say app?** "I would absolutely use that. If I could just type '3 rotis, big bowl of dal, some chicken' or send a quick voice note and have AI calculate it, I'd use it daily. It completely removes the friction of searching menus."

**9. What would keep you using it?** "It needs to be frictionless and take less than 30 seconds a day. Just let me log my food naturally without making me feel like an accountant doing math."

**10. Age and food?** Early 20s; mostly home-cooked meals.
